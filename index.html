L1–L2 香港法律制度根基 · MC 高頻
  • Sources of Law (法律來源)
    The hierarchy is: Basic Law is supreme, followed by Statute Law — Ordinances and Subsidiary Legislation — then Case Law, comprising Common Law and Equity, and Chinese Customary Law on matters such as New Territories land. Basic Law Article 8 preserves the laws previously in force, while Basic Law Article 18 lists the laws in force in the HKSAR.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    法律位階由高至低排列，附屬立法唔可以超越主體條例，否則屬 ultra vires（越權）而無效。答題時唔好將位階倒轉。
  • Common Law & Equity (普通法與衡平法)
    Common Law is judge-made law developed through precedent in an adversarial system, where the judge is passive and neutral. Equity developed to mitigate the rigidity of the common law, providing fair remedies where strict legal rights would produce injustice. Where they conflict, Equity prevails.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    普通法靠先例、法官被動中立；衡平法補救普通法嘅僵化。兩者衝突時以衡平法優先，但要先分清邊一方持有 legal interest（法律權益）、邊一方持有 equitable interest（衡平權益）。
    📌 Case: Wong Chim Ying v Cheng Kam Wing
    Facts: Matrimonial home bought with husband's money but registered in wife's name. Wife later sold it.
    Holding: Common law recognized wife as legal owner, but Equity recognized husband's equitable interest. Equitable interest prevailed.
    🇭🇰 中文：男方出錢買樓但登記女方名下，女方其後賣樓。普通法重形式，衡平法重實質公義，最終衡平權益勝出。Short Q 要寫齊邊個出錢、登記喺邊個名下、點解 Equity 介入。
  • Rule of Law (法治)
    Dicey's Three Points: (1) Supremacy of Law — no one is punishable except for a distinct breach of law, and government power must have a legal basis. (2) Equality before the Law — every person, including officials, is subject to the ordinary law and ordinary courts. (3) Constitutional principles derive from judicial decisions — rights are protected through remedies given by courts in individual cases.
    Modern views add that government acts need a legal basis, discretionary power must be restricted and reviewable, and an independent judiciary must be able to review government action.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    戴雪三點：(1) 法律至上 —— 除非明確違法，否則無人可受罰；(2) 法律面前人人平等，包括官員；(3) 憲法原則源自司法判決。現代觀點再強調限制酌情權同司法覆核。
  • Separation of Powers (三權分立)
    State power is divided into the Legislature which makes, amends and repeals laws, the Executive which implements and enforces laws, and the Judiciary which adjudicates disputes and interprets laws. The doctrine works through checks and balances — for example, judicial review lets courts check executive action, preventing any one branch from abusing power.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    立法制訂法律、行政執行法律、司法裁斷爭議，三權互相制衡。引用條文要準：Basic Law Articles 43–65（行政）、Articles 66–79（立法）、Articles 80–96（司法）。
  • Judicial Independence (司法獨立)
    In the broad sense, it means the judiciary is free from interference by the executive and legislature as an institution. In the narrow sense, it means an individual judge is free from interference in decision-making in a particular case.
    Constitutional safeguards include Basic Law Article 85 — courts exercise judicial power independently, Article 88 — appointment on the recommendation of the Judicial Officers Recommendation Commission (Cap 92), Article 89 — security of tenure, removal only for inability or misbehaviour, and Article 90 — the Chief Justice is appointed and removed with LegCo endorsement. Guiding principle: "Justice must not only be done but must also be seen to be done."
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    廣義係司法機關整體不受干擾，狹義係個別法官判案不受干擾。就算實際無偏袒，表面偏頗 appearance of bias 都足以損害公信力。
  • Court Structure (法院架構)
    From the top: Court of Final Appeal (CFA) → Court of Appeal (CA) → Court of First Instance (CFI) — the CA and CFI together form the High Court → District Court (DC): civil jurisdiction up to $3,000,000 and criminal sentencing up to 7 years' imprisonment → Magistrates' Court: up to 2 years' imprisonment and a $100,000 fine → Small Claims Tribunal: claims up to $75,000, with no legal representation to keep proceedings cheap and informal.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    數字係畀分點，要背齊：區域法院民事 $3M / 刑事 7 年、裁判法院 2 年 / $100,000、小額錢債 $75,000 兼唔准律師。另有少年法庭、勞資審裁處、土地審裁處、死因裁判法庭、家事法庭。
  • Basic Law Article 158 (基本法第 158 條 · 解釋權)
    The power of interpretation of the Basic Law is vested in the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (NPCSC). HKSAR courts are authorised to interpret on their own, in adjudicating cases, provisions within the limits of the Region's autonomy.
    But if a provision concerns affairs which are the responsibility of the Central People's Government or the relationship between the Central Authorities and the Region, and its interpretation affects the judgment, the CFA must, before making its final judgment, seek an interpretation from the NPCSC, and must follow that interpretation. Related: Basic Law Article 159 — the power of amendment belongs to the NPC.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    解釋權歸人大常委。自治範圍內條文法院可自行解釋；涉及中央事務或中央與特區關係，先要喺終局判決前提請釋法。唔好同第 159 條修改權撈亂。
  • National Security Law (《香港國安法》)
    It creates four categories of offence: Secession, Subversion, Terrorist Activities and Collusion with a Foreign Country or External Elements. The local Safeguarding National Security Ordinance 2024 adds offences including Treason, Insurrection, offences relating to State Secrets, Sabotage and External Interference.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    國安法四罪：分裂國家、顛覆國家政權、恐怖活動、勾結外國或境外勢力。2024 年《維護國家安全條例》再加叛國、叛亂、國家秘密、破壞活動、境外干預。
  • Legislative Process (立法程序)
    A bill passes through First Reading → Second Reading → Committee Stage → Third Reading, then is signed by the Chief Executive and gazetted before taking effect. Subsidiary legislation must stay within the power conferred by its parent Ordinance, otherwise it is ultra vires and invalid. Legislation is in principle not retrospective.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    一讀、二讀、委員會階段、三讀，再經行政長官簽署刊憲生效。附屬立法唔可以超越主體法例授權；法例原則上無追溯力。
Social Work Link（社工連結）： Social Workers Registration Ordinance, Cap 505, s.17 — 被裁定干犯令專業聲譽受損嘅罪行，註冊局可拒絕註冊。所以國安法唔係「同社工無關」：刑事定罪會直接影響註冊資格同專業前途。

L3 刑事法 Criminal Law元素題必考
  • Functions of Criminal Law & Prosecution (刑事法功能與檢控)
    Criminal law protects society and maintains order through deterrence, incapacitation, rehabilitation and retribution. The process is: arrest by the police → prosecution by the Department of Justice → trial by the court. The prosecution must prove every element of the offence — Actus Reus + Mens Rea.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    刑事法透過阻嚇、隔離、更生、報應保護社會。社工答題第一步永遠係拆元素，唔好一開始就講應唔應該罰。
  • Burden of Proof & Standard of Proof (舉證責任與標準)
    In criminal cases the prosecution must prove guilt beyond reasonable doubt — the highest standard, because the defendant's liberty is at stake. In civil cases the standard is only the balance of probabilities — more likely than not. This rests on the Presumption of Innocence: the accused is presumed innocent until proven guilty, so the accused does not have to prove his own innocence.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    刑事要無合理疑點，民事只需相對可能性。MC 最鍾意將兩個標準對調出題，見到 civil case 用 beyond reasonable doubt 即係錯。
  • Actus Reus (犯罪行為)
    The Actus Reus is the prohibited act specified by the offence, and it can also consist of a failure to act — an omission — where there is a legal duty to act. There is no general duty to rescue. A duty may arise from statute, contract, voluntary assumption of care, or creating a danger. Local example: OAPO Cap 212, s.27 imposes a duty on persons responsible for a child.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    一般原則係「唔救人本身唔犯法」。只有當法律責任存在，先會因不作為入罪。
    📌 Case: R v Miller — Duty Arising from Creating a Danger
    Facts: Squatter fell asleep smoking, started fire, woke up but didn't extinguish or call fire brigade. He simply moved to another room and went back to sleep.
    Holding: Once you create a danger and become aware of it, you have a duty to act. His failure to act formed the actus reus.
    🇭🇰 中文：責任唔係嚟自「佢係屋主」，而係嚟自「危險係佢造成 + 佢知情」。
    📌 Case: R v Stone & Dobinson — Voluntary Assumption of Care
    Facts: Couple took in anorexic relative, failed to summon help. Her condition deteriorated badly and she died.
    Holding: Voluntary assumption of care creates a legal duty. Their gross failure to act made them liable for manslaughter.
    🇭🇰 中文：照顧關係一旦成立，「我咩都冇做」唔係抗辯，反而正正係入罪基礎。
  • Causation (因果關係)
    The prosecution must prove the defendant's act caused the prohibited result. Step 1 — Factual causation: the 'but-for' test: but for the defendant's act, would the result have occurred? Step 2 — Legal causation: the defendant's act must remain an operative and substantial cause of the result.
    The chain can be broken by a novus actus interveniens — a free, deliberate and informed intervening act by a third party or the victim — but ordinary or even negligent medical treatment normally does not break the chain; only abnormal treatment will.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    先考「若非」測試，再考法律因果。一般甚至疏忽嘅醫療處理通常唔斷鏈，只有異常醫療處理先會斷鏈。
    📌 Case: R v Smith — Chain NOT Broken
    Facts: Stabbed victim dropped multiple times, given wrong treatment.
    Holding: Chain NOT broken, original wound still operative cause. Defendant remained liable for the death.
    🇭🇰 中文：醫療失誤就算幾差，只要原傷勢仍在發揮作用，介入行為唔會自動斷鏈。
    📌 Case: R v Jordan — Chain BROKEN
    Facts: Victim given large dose of drug he was intolerant to while recovering in hospital, and died from that reaction.
    Holding: Abnormal medical treatment BROKE the chain. It amounted to a novus actus interveniens; defendant was not liable for the death.
    🇭🇰 中文：同 R v Smith 對讀係必考位 — 分別唔係「有冇醫療失誤」，而係失誤去到 abnormal 程度、令原傷勢唔再係 operative cause 未。
    📌 Case: R v Pagett — Reasonable Self-Defence Does Not Break the Chain
    Facts: Used hostage as a shield, police shot hostage while returning fire in self-defence.
    Holding: Reasonable act of self-defence doesn't break chain; deliberate third-party act might. Defendant was liable for manslaughter.
    🇭🇰 中文：被迫、合理嘅反應係 foreseeable response，唔會斷鏈；死亡源於被告迫使對峙嘅行為。
  • Mens Rea (犯罪意圖)
    The Mens Rea is the mental element, in three descending levels: (1) Intention — the defendant aims at or knows the result is virtually certain. (2) Recklessness — the defendant foresees a risk and unjustifiably takes it. (3) Negligence — the defendant falls below the standard of the reasonable person, without necessarily foreseeing the risk; criminal liability usually requires gross negligence.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    意圖最高、罔顧次之、疏忽最低。Short Q 要按罪行要求指出需要邊一級 mens rea，唔好籠統寫「有意圖」就算。
  • Defences (抗辯)
    Common defences negating liability include Age — a child under 10 bears no criminal liability, Insanity, Automatism, Intoxication, Duress & Necessity and Self-Defence. Each defence has its own elements and evidential requirements; apply each element to the facts.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    唔係「提出咗就脫罪」。每種抗辯都有自己嘅元素同舉證要求，要逐項套事實分析，結論先有分。
    📌 Case: HKSAR v Shum Man Fai (2021) — Self-Defence: Two Limbs
    Facts / Rule: Self-defence must satisfy two limbs — (1) Necessity (honest belief), (2) Reasonableness (proportionate force).
    Holding: (1) Necessity — defendant honestly believed force was necessary to defend himself or another. (2) Reasonableness — force used was reasonable and proportionate to the threat faced. The force must not be retaliatory.
    🇭🇰 中文：自衛兩肢：先寫 honest belief（主觀），再寫 proportionate force（客觀），最後檢查有冇 retaliation 成分。威脅過咗再出手，兩肢都唔成立。
  • Classification of Offences & Theft (罪行分類與盜竊)
    Offences may be summary, indictable — serious indictable offences are tried in the CFI before a jury — or either-way. Theft Ordinance, Cap 210, s.2(1) — theft has four elements: dishonestly, appropriates, property belonging to another, with the intention of permanently depriving.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    盜竊四元素缺一不可：不誠實地、挪佔、屬於他人嘅財產、意圖永久剝奪。
答題骨（老師 L3 原稿）+ Short Q 出題陷阱 / 高分位 — L3 Criminal Law
  • 固定四步：① Identify the Issue ② Relevant Legal Concepts ③ Analysis（用概念套事實，有冇抗辯如 consent 同意？）④ Conclusion（講明立場）。
  • Causation 一定要寫齊 'but-for' test 同 novus actus interveniens，再用 R v Smith（not broken）對比 R v Jordan（abnormal treatment broke the chain）。
  • Omissions 先要指出 legal duty 來源：R v Miller 係 created the danger、R v Stone & Dobinson 係 voluntary assumption of care，冇 duty 就冇 actus reus。
  • Self-Defence 要逐肢寫 HKSAR v Shum Man Fai (2021) 嘅 necessity（honest belief）+ reasonableness（proportionate force），並明言 force must not be retaliatory。
  • 舉證標準唔好寫錯：criminal = beyond reasonable doubt，civil = balance of probabilities。
L4 成人法：家暴・OAPO・DCRVO社工實務高頻
  • Offences Against the Person Ordinance, Cap 212 (《侵害人身罪條例》第 212 章)
    The Offences Against the Person Ordinance, Cap 212 grades offences against the person by the seriousness of the harm and the offender's mental element. s.40 common assault — maximum 1 year; s.39 assault occasioning actual bodily harm (ABH) — 3 years; s.19 wounding or inflicting grievous bodily harm (GBH) — 3 years; s.17 GBH with malice and with arms — life imprisonment. At the top of the scale, s.2 murder carries life imprisonment, and s.7 manslaughter carries life imprisonment and a fine.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    Cap 212 按傷害嚴重程度同犯罪意圖分級。背刑罰要連條文一齊背：s.40 普通襲擊 1 年、s.39 ABH 3 年、s.19 GBH 3 年、s.17 惡意加武器 GBH 終身、s.2 謀殺終身、s.7 誤殺終身兼罰款。MC 最鍾意將 s.39 / s.19 / s.17 嘅刑罰對調出題。
  • Assault & Battery (襲擊與毆打)
    Assault is a display of threat causing the victim to apprehend immediate unlawful violence — no physical contact is needed, only that the victim anticipates force about to be used. Battery is the actual, intentional and unlawful use of force on another person. Common assault under s.40 can be committed by either form where there are no aggravating features. The distinction matters in analysis: identify first whether contact occurred, then grade the resulting harm as ABH or GBH.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    Assault 睇「預期」—— 令對方合理相信武力即將被使用，唔使掂到；Battery 睇「接觸」—— 實際施加非法武力。Short Q 分析次序：先分有冇接觸，再分傷害係 ABH 定 GBH，最後先落條文同刑罰。
  • Ill-treatment or Neglect of a Child — OAPO s.27 (虐待或忽略兒童)
    OAPO Cap 212, s.27 criminalises the ill-treatment or neglect of a child, with a maximum penalty ranging from 3 to 10 years' imprisonment. Although placed in the Adult Law lecture, this provision is the bridge to Child Law in L5: the same section is used to prosecute wilful assault, ill-treatment, neglect, abandonment or exposure of a child causing unnecessary suffering or injury to health. A social worker must therefore know s.27 in both contexts — as a serious offence against the person, and as a core child-protection provision.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    s.27 虐待／忽略兒童最高 3 至 10 年，係 L4 同 L5 之間嘅橋樑條文。L5 會再深入講構成元素；呢度先記住刑罰範圍同佢屬於 Cap 212。
  • Sexual Offences — Crimes Ordinance (《刑事罪行條例》性罪行)
    Under the Crimes Ordinance, s.118 rape carries life imprisonment, while s.122 indecent assault carries a maximum of 10 years. Rape and indecent assault must not be confused: they differ in the act involved and in the maximum penalty. In a domestic or intimate-partner context, forced sex after a refusal may therefore engage criminal liability under s.118 in addition to any civil remedy under the DCRVO — the two tracks are separate and can run together.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    強姦 s.118 終身、非禮 s.122 最高 10 年，唔好撈亂。婚姻或伴侶關係唔係免責理由；拒絕之後強迫性交，要同時諗刑事 s.118 同民事 DCRVO 兩條路。

    Facts: Ho Mai and Mei Li had been married for 10 years. There was verbal abuse in the marriage, and Ho Mai forced sex on Mei Li after she had refused. Their daughter overheard what happened and told the school social worker.

  • Domestic and Cohabitation Relationships Violence Ordinance, Cap 189 — Civil Remedy (《家庭及同居關係暴力條例》第 189 章・民事補救)
    The Domestic and Cohabitation Relationships Violence Ordinance, Cap 189 (DCRVO) provides a civil remedy for victims of domestic and cohabitation relationship violence, in contrast to the criminal route under Cap 212. Its centrepiece is the non-molestation order under s.3(1)(a) for an adult applicant and s.3(1)(b) for a specified minor. Under s.3(1A), the court may also direct the respondent to attend a programme aimed at changing the attitude and behaviour that led to the molestation. A civil injunction can be sought without waiting for a criminal prosecution or conviction.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    DCRVO 係民事補救，唔使等刑事檢控或定罪先申請到。不騷擾令：成人 s.3(1)(a)、指明未成年人 s.3(1)(b)；s.3(1A) 可命令答辯人參加改變態度行為嘅課程。答題要寫明申請人係成人定 specified minor，先揀得啱 sub-section。
  • Exclusion & Entry Orders; Court Considerations; Power of Arrest (遷出令／重返令・法院考慮・逮捕授權)
    Beyond a non-molestation order, s.3(1)(c)(i) empowers the court to make an exclusion or entry order for an adult, and s.3(1)(c)(ii) does the same for a specified minor — excluding the respondent from, or allowing the applicant to re-enter, the shared home. In deciding whether and how to exercise these powers, the court has regard to the conduct of the parties, the needs and financial resources of the parties, and the needs of any specified minor. Under s.5, an authorisation of arrest may be attached where actual bodily harm has been caused or is likely to be caused, so that a breach can be met with immediate arrest.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    遷出／重返令：成人 s.3(1)(c)(i)、指明未成年人 s.3(1)(c)(ii)。法院考慮三樣嘢：雙方行為、雙方需要同財政資源、指明未成年人嘅需要。s.5 逮捕授權書嘅門檻係已造成 ABH 或好可能造成 ABH。

    Application: As a cohabiting partner, Mary falls within the DCRVO's civil remedy.   • Molestation (騷擾)
    Molestation under the DCRVO is wider than physical violence. It includes assault, intercepting or following the victim, shouting obscenities, sending abusive letters, repeatedly telephoning, and pestering. The concept therefore covers a course of harassing and controlling conduct — such as the isolation in Q11 and the intimate partner violence in Q6 — and not only a single act causing injury. Where the conduct also amounts to a criminal assault or a sexual offence, the criminal law (Cap 212 / Crimes Ordinance) and the civil DCRVO remedy should be analysed in parallel.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：

  • Guardianship — Introduction under the Mental Health Ordinance (《精神健康條例》監護・引子)
    Adult Law introduces guardianship under the Mental Health Ordinance as the protective framework for adults who cannot protect themselves. A mentally incapacitated person (MIP) in this introductory sense is a mentally disordered or mentally handicapped person who is incapable of living an independent life or of guarding against serious exploitation. Guardianship is therefore a last-resort protective intervention for adults at risk, and its full definitions, the Guardianship Board and guardian powers are examined in detail in L6.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：

L5 兒童法 Child Law福利原則必背
  • Divorce — Irretrievable Breakdown & the Five Facts (離婚・婚姻無可挽救破裂與五個事實)
    Under the Matrimonial Causes Ordinance, Cap 179, s.11A, the sole ground for divorce is that the marriage has broken down irretrievably. Breakdown is proved by one of five facts: (1) adultery; (2) unreasonable behaviour; (3) separation for 1 year with the respondent's consent; (4) living apart for 2 years; (5) desertion for 1 year. The petitioner must plead and prove a fact — dissatisfaction with the marriage alone is not enough — and once divorce is engaged, the court's focus shifts to the children's welfare and financial provision.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    離婚唯一理由係 s.11A 婚姻破裂至無可挽救，再用五個事實證明：通姦、不合理行為、雙方同意分居 1 年、分居 2 年、遺棄 1 年。MC 陷阱係年期：同意分居係 1 年、無同意分居係 2 年、遺棄係 1 年，唔好對調。
  • UN Convention on the Rights of the Child & the Best Interests Principle (《兒童權利公約》與兒童最佳利益原則)
    The UN Convention on the Rights of the Child provides that a child shall not be separated from his or her parents against their will except where such separation is necessary for the best interests of the child, and that parents and others responsible for the child must protect the child from all forms of physical or mental violence, injury or abuse, neglect or negligent treatment, maltreatment or exploitation, including sexual abuse. In Hong Kong family proceedings this translates into the guiding principle that the child's best interest / welfare comes first: custody, care and access arrangements are decided by what serves the child, not by which parent has the stronger claim.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    公約兩大位：① 兒童唔應違背意願同父母分離，除非為其最佳利益而有必要；② 父母要保護兒童免受身體／精神暴力、傷害、虐待、忽略、苛待同剝削，包括性虐待。答題開頭先立 best interests 係指導原則，再套落管養同探視安排。
    📌 Application: Q10 — MIP Father & Post-divorce Co-parenting
    Facts: The father is a mentally incapacitated person (MIP), and the question concerns post-divorce co-parenting arrangements for the children.
    Application: The father's MIP status does not by itself decide custody or access. The court starts from the child's best interests, assesses the actual risk and support available, and considers co-parenting arrangements that protect the child while preserving the parent-child relationship where it is safe to do so.
    🇭🇰 中文：父親係 MIP 唔等於自動冇管養或探視權。要由兒童最佳利益出發，評估實際風險同支援，唔好用標籤代替分析 —— 呢點同 L6 嘅 MIP 定義互相呼應。
  • Welfare Principle & Welfare Checklist — Guardianship of Minors Ordinance, Cap 13, s.3(1) (福利原則與福利清單・《未成年人監護條例》第 3(1) 條)
    Under the Guardianship of Minors Ordinance, Cap 13, s.3(1), the child's welfare / best interests are the first and paramount consideration in custody and upbringing proceedings. The court considers the child's ascertainable wishes, assessed according to the child's age and understanding, together with information such as reports of the Director of Social Welfare. The welfare checklist directs attention to: the child's own wishes and feelings; the child's physical, emotional and educational needs; the child's relationships with each parent and with other persons; the likely effect of any change in circumstances; the child's age, maturity and background; any harm the child has suffered or is at risk of suffering, including family violence; each parent's capability of meeting the child's needs; and the range of powers available to the court. The exercise is not a comparison of which parent's rights are greater — the parents' claims are subordinate to what serves the child.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    Cap 13 s.3(1)：兒童最佳利益係首要同最重要考慮。福利清單要逐項對事實：兒童意願感受（按年齡同理解能力衡量）、身心及教育需要、與父母及他人嘅關係、環境改變嘅影響、年齡成熟度同背景、已受或可能受嘅傷害（包括家庭暴力）、父母照顧能力、法院可用權力範圍；再加社署署長報告等資料。陷阱位：唔可以寫成單純比較父親定母親邊個權利大 —— 父母權利要讓路畀兒童福利。
  • Maintenance for Children (兒童贍養費)
    On divorce, the court may order maintenance for a child by way of periodical payments or a lump sum. The obligation ordinarily lasts until the child reaches 18, but it may continue beyond 18 if the child is still in education or training. Maintenance is part of the welfare analysis, not a bargaining chip between the parents: the question is always what provision the child needs, for how long, and in what form.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    贍養可以定期付款或一筆過。一般到 18 歲為止；如果滿 18 歲後仍在接受教育或培訓，可以延長。寫答案要交代形式（periodical / lump sum）同期數去到幾時。
  • Separation and Maintenance Orders Ordinance — Grounds (《分居及贍養令條例》申請理由)
    The Separation and Maintenance Orders Ordinance allows a spouse to seek a separation and maintenance order on specified grounds: the other spouse has been convicted of an assault on the applicant of an aggravated character; desertion; persistent cruelty to the spouse or the children; failure to provide reasonable maintenance; insisting on sexual intercourse while suffering from venereal disease; compelling the spouse into prostitution; or being a habitual drunkard or drug addict. Each ground must be matched to the facts — persistent cruelty, for example, requires a course of conduct, not a single quarrel.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    七個理由要背齊：加重襲擊配偶定罪、遺棄、持續虐待配偶或兒童、冇提供合理贍養、患性病仍堅持性交、迫使配偶賣淫、慣性酗酒或吸毒。Persistent cruelty 要寫「持續」性質，一次爭吵唔夠。
  • Child Abuse — Definition, Types & Investigation (虐兒・定義、類型與調查)
    Child abuse is any act or omission that endangers or impairs a child's physical, psychological or emotional health or development. Its types include physical and psychological abuse, sexual abuse, gross neglect, and child prostitution. Suspected cases are investigated through referrals, a child assessment, and a Multi-Disciplinary Case Conference (MDCC), where professionals from different disciplines share information and decide together on the protection and welfare plan for the child.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    定義要寫齊 act or omission（作為或不作為）+ endangers or impairs 健康或發展。類型：身體／心理、性虐待、嚴重忽略、兒童賣淫。流程三步：轉介 → 兒童評估 → 多專業個案會議 MDCC，唔好漏咗社工喺 MDCC 嘅角色。

    Holding points (as stated in Q7): The facts as stated in the source engage gross neglect and physical abuse under the child-abuse definition, OAPO s.27 ill-treatment or neglect, and the PCJO s.34(2) care-or-protection framework; the case is used in the course to examine identification, assessment and multi-disciplinary handling of serious child abuse.

  • Sexual Offences Against Children — Crimes Ordinance (《刑事罪行條例》涉及兒童嘅性罪行)
    The Crimes Ordinance protects children and mentally incapacitated persons with heavy penalties: unlawful sexual intercourse with a girl under 13 — life imprisonment; with a girl under 16 — 5 years; unlawful sexual intercourse with a mentally incapacitated woman — 10 years; gross indecency towards a child under 16 — 10 years; causing or encouraging child prostitution — 10 years; and buggery — life imprisonment. The age bands are the key examination point: the younger the child, the heavier the maximum, and a child's apparent agreement does not make the act lawful.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    按年齡分級背：13 歲以下女童性交終身、16 歲以下 5 年、與精神上無行為能力女子性交 10 年、向 16 歲以下兒童粗獷猥褻 10 年、促使兒童賣淫 10 年、肛交終身。年齡帶同刑罰係 MC 最愛對調嘅位。
  • OAPO s.27 — Ill-treatment or Neglect of a Child (《侵害人身罪條例》第 27 條)
    OAPO Cap 212, s.27 makes it an offence to wilfully assault, ill-treat, neglect, abandon or expose a child in a manner likely to cause unnecessary suffering or injury to health. The slides give concrete examples of neglect: failing to provide adequate food, clothing or lodging for the child, or knowingly failing to take steps to secure help when the carer cannot provide them. The offence therefore covers omissions as much as positive acts — a carer who lets a child starve cannot defend by saying he never struck the child.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    s.27 五個動詞：wilfully assault, ill-treat, neglect, abandon, expose，結果係令兒童不必要受苦或健康受損。例子要識舉：冇提供足夠食物、衣物、住宿，或者明知自己提供唔到都唔去求助 —— 不作為一樣入罪，呼應 L3 omissions 嘅法律責任。
  • Child in Need of Care or Protection — PCJO Cap 213, s.34(2) (需要照顧或保護嘅兒童)
    Under the Protection of Children and Juveniles Ordinance, Cap 213, s.34(2), a child or juvenile is in need of care or protection if: (a) he has been or is being assaulted, ill-treated, neglected or sexually abused; (b) his health, development or welfare has been neglected or avoidably impaired; (c) his health, development or welfare is likely to be neglected or avoidably impaired; or (d) he is beyond control to the extent that harm may be caused to him or others. The Director of Social Welfare (DSW) may act as legal guardian, and the court may make a care order or a supervision order — supervision may last up to 3 years, with the supervisor's role to advise, assist and befriend the child and family.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：

L6 精神健康・遺囑認證・法律援助定義題溫床
  • Mental Disorder — MHO Cap 136, s.2 (精神紊亂・《精神健康條例》第 2 條)
    Mental Health Ordinance, Cap 136, s.2 defines mental disorder as: (a) mental illness; (b) arrested or incomplete development of mind, including a significant impairment of intelligence and social functioning associated with abnormally aggressive or seriously irresponsible conduct; (c) psychopathic disorder; or (d) any other disorder or disability of the mind which does not amount to mental handicap. The definition is a legal gateway, not a medical diagnosis: only a person who falls within s.2 can be made subject to the Ordinance's reception, detention and guardianship powers.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    精神紊亂四類：精神病、心智發展受阻或不完全（智能同社交功能顯著受損＋異常侵略或嚴重不負責任行為）、精神病理障礙、其他未達弱智程度嘅心智障礙。記住最後一句：唔屬於 mental handicap 嗰啲先落 (d)，兩個定義唔好撈亂。
  • Mental Handicap, Mental Incapacity, MIP, Patient & Relative (弱智・精神上無行為能力・MIP・病人・親屬)
    Mental handicap means sub-average general intellectual functioning with deficiencies in adaptive behaviour — an IQ of 70 or below on the Wechsler scales. Mental incapacity means mental disorder or mental handicap. A mentally incapacitated person (MIP) means, for Part II purposes, a person who is incapable by reason of mental incapacity of managing and administering his property and affairs — and otherwise means a patient or a mentally handicapped person. A patient is a person suffering or appearing to be suffering from a mental disorder. A relative must be aged 18 or above and includes a spouse, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, uncle or aunt, nephew or niece, cousin, and a person residing with the MIP.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    定義鏈要順序背：mental handicap（IQ ≤ 70 韋氏量表＋適應行為缺損）→ mental incapacity = mental disorder or mental handicap → MIP（Part II 指不能管理財產事務；其他情況指 patient 或弱智人士）。Relative 要 18 歲以上，同住者都計，呢點同 s.59N 邊個可以申請監護直接相關。
  • Part II — Management of Property & Affairs (第 II 部・財產及事務管理)
    Part II of the MHO deals with the property and affairs of an MIP who cannot manage them himself. s.9 gives the court power to examine the position; s.10 sets out the court's powers over the MIP's property and affairs; and under s.11, where the person is incapable, the court may appoint a committee to act for him. The Part II route is about money and property — it is different from Part IVB guardianship, which is about personal and welfare decisions.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    Part II 管錢銀財產：s.9 查核權、s.10 法院權力、s.11 委任 committee（產業受託監管人）。最常見陷阱係將 Part II 同 Part IVB 監護撈亂：一個管財產、一個管個人福利決定，答題要先分清題目問邊樣。
  • Part III — Reception, Detention & Treatment (第 III 部・接收及羈留)
    Part III balances treatment against liberty. A s.30 voluntary patient is admitted on his own application and is free in principle to leave. Under s.31(1B), a person may be removed and detained for observation on the opinion of one medical officer, through an order of a District judge or magistrate. Under s.36, a certified patient may be detained for treatment, supported by two medical officers.     Part IV / IVA establishes the Mental Health Review Tribunal (see s.59E), which reviews the detention of patients.     A guardianship application under s.59M must be founded on the written reports of 2 registered medical practitioners, and guardianship must be in the interests of the welfare of the MIP or for the protection of other persons. Under s.59N, the applicants who may apply are a relative, a social worker, a registered medical practitioner, or a public officer of the Social Welfare Department. A social enquiry report is prepared under s.59P, and under s.59S a guardian must be a willing adult who is capable of providing care and has no conflict of interest.     A guardian's powers are limited and specific: to decide the MIP's place of residence; to consent to medical or dental treatment on the MIP's behalf; and to manage a limited amount of money — a maximum of HK$10,500 per month.   • Probate, Wills & Intestacy (遺囑認證・平安紙・無遺囑繼承)
    Probate is the court process of proving the validity and authenticity of a will; the executor named in the will obtains a grant of probate to administer the estate. The key instruments are a Will (平安紙), an Enduring Power of Attorney under Cap 501, and an Advance Directive. L7 侵權法 Tort — 疏忽係重心四元素必考
  • What is a Tort? (侵權法是甚麼)
    A tort is a civil wrong: a breach of a legal duty which affects an individual's interests, and it belongs to private law. It differs from public law, from criminal law — which punishes offenders and protects the public interest — and from contract law, where the obligations arise from voluntary agreement between the parties.
    Its functions are to protect rights and to compensate loss. The interests protected are: personal interests (bodily integrity, and reputation, protected through defamation), property interests (the integrity of property, the enjoyment of land, protected through nuisance, and intangible interests such as passing off and intellectual property), and economic interests (pure economic loss, and deceit). Relief is usually damages, but an injunction may also be granted.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    侵權係違反法律責任、影響個人利益嘅民事過錯，屬私法。唔好同刑事法撈亂：刑事法處罰罪犯、保障公眾利益；侵權法保障個人權利同補償損失。救濟以損害賠償為主，亦可申請禁制令。
  • Negligence: The Four Elements (疏忽四元素)
    To succeed in negligence, the claimant must prove four elements in order: (1) Duty of Care — the defendant owed the claimant a duty to take care; (2) Breach of Duty — the defendant fell below the standard of the reasonable person; (3) Causation — the breach caused the damage; and (4) Damage — the claimant suffered loss of a kind the law will compensate. If any one element fails, the whole claim fails.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    四元素要順序逐個證明，缺一不可。Short Q 分析任何疏忽情境，都要逐個元素套事實，唔好跳過 Duty 直接講賠償；Q16 BGCA 案正正係用呢四元素逐項拆解。
  • Duty of Care (謹慎責任)
    A duty of care exists within certain established classes of relationship, such as employer–employee, doctor–patient and school–pupils. Outside those classes, the court asks whether the claimant belonged to a class of persons who would foreseeably suffer loss from the defendant's conduct, and whether the kind of damage suffered was foreseeable.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    先睇關係係咪屬於已知類別（僱主—僱員、醫生—病人、學校—學生）；唔係嘅話，就要證明原告屬於可預見會受損失嘅一類人，而且所受損失嘅「種類」係可預見嘅。
    📌 Case: Donoghue v Stevenson [1932] AC 562 — The Neighbour Principle
    Facts: A manufacturer sold ginger beer to a retailer. The purchaser's friend consumed it and found a decomposing snail in the bottle; she suffered illness. She had no contract with the manufacturer, so she could not sue in contract.
    Holding: The manufacturer owed a duty of care to the ultimate consumer, where the product reaches the consumer in the form in which it left him, with no reasonable possibility of intermediate examination. This case founded the neighbour principle: you must take reasonable care to avoid acts or omissions you can reasonably foresee would injure persons so closely and directly affected by your act that you ought to have them in contemplation.
    🇭🇰 中文：冇合約關係唔代表冇責任。產品去到最終消費者手上時冇機會中途檢查，製造商就對消費者負謹慎責任 —— 呢個就係鄰居原則嘅來源。
    📌 Case: The Wagon Mound (No.1) — The Kind of Damage Must Be Foreseeable
    Facts: Furnace oil was spilled from a vessel. The damage that was foreseeable from such a spillage was pollution of the water, not fire damage.
    Holding: The fire damage was not actionable, because fire was not the kind of damage that was foreseeable; only damage of a foreseeable kind can be recovered.
    🇭🇰 中文：漏油可預見嘅係污染，唔係火災。損失種類唔同、唔可預見，就唔賠 —— 呢個位同 Jolley（方式唔使預見）、Smith v Leech Brain（程度唔使預見）要分清楚。
  • The Caparo Three-Stage Test (Caparo 三步測試)
    In novel situations, the court applies the three-stage test in Caparo Industries plc v Dickman [1990] 2 AC 605: (1) the damage must be reasonably foreseeable; (2) there must be a relationship of close proximity between the parties — which may be physical, circumstantial or causal; and (3) it must be fair, just and reasonable to impose a duty of care. All three stages must be satisfied.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    新類型個案用 Caparo 三步：可預見性、關係密切（身體上、情境上或因果上）、施加責任屬公平公正合理。三步要齊，淨係可預見並唔足夠。
    📌 Case: Luen Hing Fat Coating & Finishing Factory Ltd v Waan Chuen Ming (2011) 14 HKCFAR 14
    Facts: A factory operator lent pallet jacks and a bearing trolley to an independent contractor's employee, to reinstall a 1.5-ton machine part by an unsafe method. The supervisor knew of the method but did not stop it or warn him; the part fell and crushed both his legs.
    Holding: A duty of care was owed. The operation was dangerous and harm was foreseeable; the claimant was on the defendant's premises using loaned equipment; the defendant took a positive role in the operation and had chosen the contractor who had to borrow equipment; and personal safety was at risk.
    🇭🇰 中文：傷者唔係自己員工都好：被告借出設備、積極參與、明知方法唔安全又唔阻止 —— proximity 同 fair, just and reasonable 都成立，人身安全唔可以為慳錢犧牲。
  • Breach of Duty (違反謹慎責任)
    The classic definition comes from Blyth v Birmingham Waterworks: negligence is the omission to do something which a reasonable man would do, or doing something which a prudent and reasonable man would not do. The standard is objective — the reasonable and prudent man in the defendant's position. The defendant's own weaknesses or inexperience are irrelevant (a learner driver is judged by the standard of a competent driver), but a higher standard applies where the defendant holds himself out as having specialist skill.
    In deciding whether the standard was met, the court weighs: the likelihood of harm, the defendant's knowledge of the risk, the vulnerability of the victims (for example, children), the magnitude of the consequences, the cost and practicability of precautions, and common practice. Overall, the court balances the risk against the effectiveness, expense and inconvenience of the precautions that could have been taken.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    標準係客觀嘅「合理謹慎人」，唔睇被告個人弱點或經驗不足；但自稱專家就要用專家標準。答題要逐個因素套事實，唔好淨係寫「佢唔小心」。
    📌 Case: Bolton v Stone [1951] AC 850 — Likelihood of Harm
    Facts / Rule: Cricket balls were hit over a 17-foot fence only 6 times in 30 years. Where the likelihood of harm is that small, the reasonable person is not necessarily in breach for failing to take further precautions: the likelihood and magnitude of the risk are weighed against the cost and practicability of preventing it.
    🇭🇰 中文：30 年先飛出 6 次，風險細到合理人唔一定要額外防範。呢個案係用嚟講 likelihood of harm 呢個因素，唔係話「有人受傷就一定有 breach」。
  • Causation (因果關係)
    Factual causation is tested by the 'but-for' test: but for the defendant's breach, would the damage have occurred? A material contribution to the damage may also suffice. Legal causation asks whether the chain of causation was broken by a novus actus interveniens — an intervening act, which may be a natural event, the act of a third party, or the plaintiff's own act. Whether the chain is broken is ultimately a matter of common sense.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    先考事實因果（but-for test），再考法律因果（有冇新介入行為斷鏈）。同 L3 刑事法嘅 causation 框架相同，但呢度係民事索償，舉證標準係 balance of probabilities。
  • Damage & Remoteness (損失與遙遠性)
    The kind of damage must be foreseeable, but the manner in which it occurs need not be, and the extent of the damage need not be foreseeable either. Under the thin skull (eggshell skull) rule, the defendant takes the claimant as he finds him: if the claimant has a hidden vulnerability, the defendant is liable for the full extent of the harm once a foreseeable kind of injury is shown.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    三個位要分清：種類（kind）要可預見、方式（manner）唔使、程度（extent）唔使。以下兩個案例正正係考呢個分別。
    📌 Case: Jolley v Sutton LBC [2000] 1 WLR 1082 — Manner Need Not Be Foreseeable
    Facts: A rotten, abandoned boat was left on the defendant's land. Two boys aged 13 and 14 meddled with it and lifted it up; the boat fell on one of them, causing paraplegia.
    Holding: It was foreseeable that children would meddle with the boat and be at risk of physical injury, so the defendant was liable — the kind of damage (physical injury) was foreseeable even though the precise manner in which it happened was not.
    🇭🇰 中文：細路會搞隻爛船、會受傷，呢個「種類」可預見；隻船點跌法唔使預見，被告照賠。
    📌 Case: Smith v Leech Brain & Co [1962] 2 QB 405 — Thin Skull Rule
    Facts: Molten metal burnt a worker's lip at work. The burn triggered a cancer, and the worker died three years later.
    Holding: The burn was a foreseeable kind of injury, so the defendant was liable for the death. Under the thin skull rule, the defendant takes the claimant as he finds him — the extent of the damage need not be foreseeable.
    🇭🇰 中文：燒傷本身可預見就夠；工人本身易生癌係佢嘅「薄頭骨」，被告要為最終死亡負責，唔可以話「我冇預見會死人」。
  • Defences (抗辯)
    (1) Contributory negligence: under the Law Amendment and Reform (Consolidation) Ordinance, Cap 23, s.21, where the claimant is partly at fault, damages are reduced to such extent as the court thinks just and equitable, having regard to the claimant's share in the responsibility, including causative potency and relative blameworthiness — this is only a partial defence. (2) Voluntary assumption of risk (volenti): the claimant had full knowledge of the nature and extent of the risk and voluntarily consented to run it — a complete defence. (3) Illegality: a claimant cannot recover damages for loss consequent on his own criminal act — also a complete defence.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    MC 最鍾意考「部分定完全」：共分疏忽只係按比例扣減賠償；自願承擔風險同非法性先係完全抗辯，成立就一蚊都冇。volenti 仲要證明原告知悉風險嘅性質同程度兼自願同意，淨係「知道有危險」唔足夠。
  • Occupiers' Liability (佔用人責任)
    Under the Occupiers' Liability Ordinance, Cap 314, s.3, an occupier owes the common duty of care to all his visitors: to take such care as is reasonable in all the circumstances to see that the visitor will be reasonably safe in using the premises for the purposes for which he is invited or permitted to be there. An occupier is a person with a sufficient degree of control over the premises — owners, tenants and contractors may all qualify, and there may be more than one occupier. A visitor is a person who enters with express or implied permission; trespassers are not visitors.
    The duty covers negligent acts and omissions — for example, an occupier may be liable over a slippery floor that must be mopped, even if the occupier did not create the liquid. A warning does not automatically absolve the occupier: under s.3(4)(a), the warning must be enough to enable the visitor to be reasonably safe, judged by its placement, how it was brought to the visitor's attention, and its specificity. Finally, distinguish the occupancy duty (the state or static condition of the premises, e.g. a defect on the floor or a slippery staircase) from the activity duty (an activity carried on at the premises, e.g. students hitting a goalpost while playing soccer).
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    貼咗警告牌唔等於免責 —— 警告要足以令訪客合理安全，要睇位置、點引起注意、夠唔夠具體。仲要分清 occupancy duty 睇處所狀態，activity duty 睇活動本身，兩者唔好撈亂。
  • Nuisance (妨擾)
    Private nuisance is a condition or activity which unduly interferes with another person's use or enjoyment of land, in three forms: (1) encroachment onto the neighbour's land; (2) direct physical injury to the neighbour's land; and (3) interference with the neighbour's quiet enjoyment of his land — water leakage between flats is a common example. Public nuisance is a state of affairs endangering the lives, safety, health, property or comfort of the public, or obstructing the public in the exercise of common rights; an individual claimant must additionally show particular injury of a foreseeable type, and the defendant must have known or ought to have known of the nuisance.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    私人妨擾要證明不當干擾土地嘅使用或享受；公眾妨擾影響嘅係公眾，個人原告仲要額外證明自己受咗特別損害，而且損害種類可預見、被告知道或應該知道。
    📌 Case: Leung Tsang Hung v Incorporated Owners of Kwok Wing House (2007) 10 HKCFAR 480
    Facts / Rule: The leading Hong Kong authority on public nuisance cited in the lecture: a public nuisance is a state of affairs endangering the lives, safety, health, property or comfort of the public, or obstructing the public in exercising common rights; the claimant must suffer particular injury of a foreseeable type, and the defendant must have known, or ought to have known, of the risk.
    🇭🇰 中文：引呢個案要寫齊公眾妨擾嘅構成：影響公眾 + 原告特別損害 + 種類可預見 + 被告知情或應知情，缺一不可。
  • Intentional Torts: Trespass (故意侵權：侵犯)
    Trespass to the person takes three forms: assault — an act causing the victim to apprehend immediate unlawful force; battery — the actual infliction of unlawful force on another; and false imprisonment — the complete restraint of the victim's freedom of movement without lawful excuse. All three are actionable per se: no proof of damage is needed. The same family of torts includes trespass to land and wrongful interference with goods.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    同 L4 分清：刑事嗰邊 assault / battery 睇 OAPO 刑罰；侵權嗰邊重點係 actionable per se —— 冇實際損失都可以告。False imprisonment 要係「完全」限制行動自由，先至成立。
  • Vicarious Liability (轉承責任)
    Vicarious liability is legal responsibility imposed on one person for the torts of another, regardless of the fault of the non-tortfeasor. Two questions must be answered: first, is the relationship between the defendant and the tortfeasor one that can give rise to vicarious liability — classically, employer–employee; and secondly, is the tort sufficiently closely connected with that relationship?
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    轉承責任唔睇被告自己有冇錯，睇兩樣嘢：關係（係咪僱主—僱員）同關連（侵權行為同職務有冇密切關連）。社工機構為員工嘅侵權行為負責，正正係呢個原則。
    📌 Case: Poon Chau Nam v Yim Siu Cheung (2007) 10 HKCFAR 156 — The Relationship Test
    Facts / Rule: The modern approach to identifying an employment relationship weighs all the circumstances: the degree of control exercised, who bears the chance of profit and risk of loss, whether the worker is part of the organisation, whether he works on his own account or for the employer's business, who provides the equipment, the tax arrangements, the parties' own view of the relationship, and the structure of the trade.
    🇭🇰 中文：唔好淨睇合約寫「自僱」就算，要逐個因素睇實質關係：控制程度、盈虧、設備、稅務、係咪組織一部分。
    📌 Case: Ming An Insurance Co (HK) Ltd v Ritz-Carlton Ltd (2002) 5 HKCFAR 569 — The Close Connection Test
    Facts / Rule: Even where an employment relationship exists, there must be a close connection between the employment and the tort. Relevant considerations include the nature of the employment, the exposure of the public to risk created by the work, and whether the employee was acting in the employer's interests or solely for himself.
    🇭🇰 中文：員工純粹為自己目的行事（solely for himself），關連就可能唔成立；係執行職務、令公眾暴露於風險之中，僱主就要負責。
  • Remedies & Limitation Periods (救濟與時限)
    The main remedy is damages, in several forms: general damages (for pain, suffering and loss of amenity), special damages (quantifiable financial loss), aggravated damages, and exemplary (punitive) damages. An injunction may also be granted to stop a continuing wrong. Under the Limitation Ordinance, Cap 347: s.27 allows 3 years for personal injury claims; s.4 allows 6 years for actions founded on tort generally; and 12 years for an action upon a specialty (a deed).
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    時限數字係 MC 畀分位：人身傷害 3 年（s.27）、一般侵權 6 年（s.4）、契據 12 年，唔好對調。
  • Social Work Application: Outdoor Activities & Risk Assessment (社工應用：戶外活動與風險評估 · Q16)
    For a social worker organising activities, risk management follows five steps: (1) identify the hazard; (2) identify the person who would suffer; (3) assess the risk; (4) document (record) the results; and (5) review. In practice this means a site visit and route design beforehand, considering location and weather, an activity rundown and notice, guardian consent signatures, health declaration forms and participant information, proper staffing establishments, insurance (third-party liability and accident insurance), and clear safety procedures if an incident happens.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    五步要背齊次序：辨識危害 → 誰會受害 → 評估風險 → 記錄結果 → 覆檢。Q16 正正係考「機構做漏邊幾步」：冇試橋承重、檢查站太近橋、冇派人駐橋、冇警告牌。
    📌 Case: Fong Chong Chuen v The Boys' and Girls' Clubs Association of Hong Kong (DCPI 548/2018) — Q16
    Facts: The plaintiff was injured in an outdoor activity organised by the BGCA when a bridge collapsed under the combined weight of three young men crossing it at the same time. The bridge was an old piece of concrete weakened by weathering and material fatigue; Checkpoint 2 had been set up too close to the bridge, and no personnel were stationed at the bridge.
    Holding: The BGCA was liable in negligence. As organiser it owed a duty of care which included testing or ascertaining whether the bridge could hold at least three participants before the activity, and ensuring that no more than two people crossed at the same time. It breached that duty by failing to warn the plaintiff of the dangers of walking on the bridge (which were not obvious to him), failing to take reasonable care to run the event in a reasonably safe way, failing to design out unnecessary dangers, and failing to place sufficient warning signs. Had it tested the bridge, it would have redesigned the route or controlled the crossing, and the injury could have been avoided. The latent defect defence failed: following Sanfield Building Contractors Ltd v Li Kai Cheong (2003) 6 HKCFAR 207, the defendant had to adduce evidence of an alternative cause due to a latent defect, and it did not discharge that evidential burden.
    🇭🇰 中文：橋塌嘅原因係三人總重量超出承重。機構嘅責任包括事前測試承重同控制人數；「潛在缺陷」抗辯要由被告舉證證明有另一個成因，舉唔出就失敗。答 Q16 要逐項指出邊啲作為／不作為低過標準，再寫返五步點樣預防。

  • Duty 要寫齊 Caparo 三步原文：reasonable foreseeability、close proximity、fair, just and reasonable；淨寫 Donoghue 鄰居原則並唔足夠。
  • Remoteness 三個位唔好撈亂：Wagon Mound 係「種類」要可預見（污染，唔係火災）；Jolley 係「方式」唔使預見；Smith v Leech Brain 係「程度」唔使預見（thin skull rule）。

L8 人權 Human RightsShort Q 熱門
  • What are Human Rights? (人權是甚麼)
    Human rights are the most fundamental rights a person has. They exist to give every person protection in all areas of life, and in nature they are moral rights; the International Bill of Human Rights formulates the basic standards in international law. Two defining features must always be stated: human rights are inalienable — they cannot be taken away or seriously limited — and universal — they are the same for all persons everywhere, regardless of age, race, wealth or disability, simply because a person is a human being. All human beings are equal, and human rights do not come and go.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    定義題必寫 inalienable（不可剝奪）同 universal（普遍）兩個特質，同埋理據：因為係人，所以享有，唔取決於年齡、種族、貧富或殘疾。人權本質係道德權利，呢點會帶出下一節「未納入法律前法院唔會強制執行」。
  • Human Rights as Values & as Demands (人權作為價值與訴求)
    Human rights operate on two levels. As values, they are capable of implementation within different value systems, and their essential elements are recognisable everywhere. As demands, they are claims made by the powerless against power holders — primarily the state and its agents, but also business, religious leaders and professionals. The point of the demand is that the weaker person be respected, and that those holding power use it responsibly.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    社工角度最重要係第二層：人權係弱者向掌權者提出嘅訴求。掌權者唔止國家，仲包括商界、宗教領袖同專業人員 —— 社工自己都係被要求負責任用權嘅一方。
  • Origins & Development (起源與發展)
    The idea has long roots: the Greek city states, and Buddhist and Confucian thinkers, all developed notions of human dignity and right conduct. In the 19th century came the anti-slavery movement, the Red Cross, and the Hague and Geneva Conventions. The modern era of human rights began with the Universal Declaration of Human Rights 1948, approved by the UN General Assembly on 10 December — the date now marked as Human Rights Day.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    唔好答「人權係西方發明」：來源包括希臘城邦、佛教同儒家思想。現代人權時代以 1948 年《世界人權宣言》為起點，12 月 10 日係人權日，MC 會考年份同日期。
  • Human Rights in Everyday Life (日常生活中的人權議題)
    Human rights are not abstract: they arise in practical, everyday issues — discrimination (on grounds of sex, race, disability and age), work (pay, conditions and trade unions), legal and political questions (crime, security and justice), and access to health and education services. For social workers, these are the settings in which clients' rights are actually won or lost.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    Short Q 若問「人權同社工有咩關係」，就用呢四組日常議題作答：歧視、工作、法律與政治、醫療與教育服務，唔好淨係背公約年份。
  • Moral Rights vs Legal Rights (道德權利與法律權利)
    A legal right is enforceable in court: if Appel owns a home, that is his legal right, and if Brian enters without his agreement, Brian breaks the law and the court can provide a remedy. A moral right is less strong: the court will not enforce it — for example, a father who does not pay rent for a daughter squatting in his property breaches a moral duty, and the consequence is social disapproval only. Yet the right to adequate housing is a human right in international law, and the ultimate duty holder for it is the state.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    分別在於可否由法院執行：法律權利有法院救濟；道德權利被違反只會招來社會非議。但適足住房係國際法上嘅人權，最終責任人係國家，唔係個別家人。
  • Implementation: The Responsibility of the State (落實：國家的責任)
    Primary responsibility for implementing human rights rests with each state. By ratifying a convention, a state commits itself both morally and in international law. Provisions of the UDHR or of treaties may over time become part of international customary law, binding on all countries whether or not they have ratified. International standards may also be embodied in domestic law, which is when they become directly enforceable at home.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    落實嘅主責在國家。批約＝道德上同國際法上嘅承諾；條文仲可以演變成國際習慣法，約束所有國家；寫入本地法律之後，先至可以直接喺本地法院援引。
  • The Liberal View: J.S. Mill (自由主義觀點)
    In the 19th-century Western liberal view associated with J.S. Mill, human rights exist to protect individuals from overweening state power, and the state should support individual freedom. Restriction of freedom is legitimate only to protect individuals from harm to others. This emphasis on rights is a product of Western liberal thinking, and it is naturally appealing to the powerless.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    Mill 嘅核心係 harm principle：限制自由唯一正當理由係防止傷害他人。呢種以權利為本嘅思想源於西方自由主義，對弱勢者特別有吸引力。
  • Duty-Based Societies (義務本位社會)
    Not every society is rights-based. In duty-based societies, basic values are upheld differently: a person's status in the community, and his dignity, are secured by performing his duties responsibly. For example, China refuses to rate civil and political rights ahead of economic, social and cultural rights such as adequate food and housing.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    對比位：自由主義以「權利」為尊嚴來源；義務本位社會以「負責任地履行崗位義務」為尊嚴來源。中國唔將公民及政治權利排喺食物、住房等經濟社會文化權利之上，係課堂例子。
  • The Modern International Framework (現代國際框架)
    After the Second World War the United Nations was formed, and the UDHR 1948 set the benchmark — it is not a code of law, but a standard against which states are measured. Binding covenants followed in 1966: the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR) and the International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights (ICESCR). Other conventions address specific groups, including conventions on disability and on discrimination against women.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    UDHR 係宣言、benchmark，本身唔係法律；1966 年兩大公約先至係有約束力嘅條約。ICCPR 管公民政治權利，ICESCR 管經濟社會文化權利，唔好調轉。
  • Europe: The ECHR & Domestic Effect (歐洲人權公約與本地效力)
    In Europe, the Council of Europe produced the European Convention on Human Rights (ECHR), signed in 1950 and in force from 1953, with its court headquartered in Strasbourg. Note carefully: the ECHR is not the European Union. Before the Human Rights Act 1998, complaining to the European Court of Human Rights meant taking the UK government to court in Strasbourg, because the Convention had no direct effect in domestic law.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    兩個常見錯誤：ECHR 唔係歐盟（係 Council of Europe）；1998 年之前，公約權利喺英國本地法院用唔到，要遠赴 Strasbourg 告政府。原因就係下面 Brind 案講嘅國會至上。
    📌 Case: R v Secretary of State for the Home Department, ex p Brind [1991] — Lord Bridge
    Facts / Rule: Although the Convention bound the UK at Strasbourg, it had no effect in national law because of parliamentary supremacy: a treaty is not justiciable in domestic courts unless and until it is incorporated by Parliament. Lord Bridge's statement in Brind is the classic authority for this position.
    🇭🇰 中文：條約簽咗唔等於本地法院用到 —— 要經國會立法納入（incorporation）先至得。呢個原則同 Q13 Lubiano 案「國際公約要經立法本地化」係同一個道理。
  • Human Rights Act 1998 (《人權法》1998)
    After 1998, all UK laws must comply with the ECHR, and English courts can themselves adjudicate Convention rights. But the Act has real constraints: it applies to public bodies, not private individuals; applicants must first attempt all other legal remedies; and the court can only declare that a decision was wrong in law for a particular reason — it is then for the public body to correct it.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    1998 年之後唔使再去 Strasbourg，本地法院可以審。但要記住三個限制：只管公營機構、要先用盡其他補救、法院只指出決定喺法律上錯，改正係公營機構嘅事。
  • Judicial Review (司法覆核)
    Judicial review invites the court to supervise public decision-making: the court may order a local authority to act (mandamus) or order it not to act (prohibition). Crucially, JR is concerned with the process by which a decision was arrived at, not with the merits of the decision itself. The lecture example is the concurrent appointment of residence masters, which raised the inappropriate use of public funding.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    JR 睇程序，唔睇決定本身啱唔啱；mandamus 係命令去做，prohibition 係禁止去做，兩個拉丁名唔好調轉。L9 Kong Yun-ming 正正係用 JR 挑戰社署署長嘅決定。
  • Hong Kong: Protection & Restriction (香港：保障與限制)
    In Hong Kong, human rights are protected through a combination of the Sino-British Joint Declaration, the Basic Law, the Bill of Rights Ordinance, judicial review, the mass media, and the civil community. Against these protections stand statutory restrictions, such as those imposed by the Public Order Ordinance — the tension between protection and restriction is a recurring examination theme.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    香港框架要成組背：聯合聲明、基本法、人權法案條例、司法覆核、傳媒、公民社會；限制例子用《公安條例》。答題要兩邊都寫，先顯得平衡。
  • ECHR Articles & Social Work Relevance (公約條文與社工相關性)
    Article 2 — right to life (euthanasia, abortion); Article 3 — prohibition of torture (punishment of children, standards of care in homes, treatment in psychiatric hospitals and prisons); Article 5 — liberty and security of the person (detention, capacity to consent to hospital admission, asylum seekers, political interference in court sentences); Article 6 — right to a fair trial (arrest, fair trial, discharge from prison or secure hospital); Article 8 — respect for private and family life (checking correspondence, surveillance, the rights of patients to nominate a relative); Article 10 — freedom of expression (confidentiality, press coverage); Article 12 — the right to marry and found a family (the position of transsexuals, changing birth data); Article 13 — right to an effective remedy (denying children access to remedies).
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    唔好淨背條文編號，要連社工情境一齊背：Art 3 連院舍照顧標準、Art 5 連同意入院能力、Art 8 連查閱服務使用者通信同保密，呢啲正正係 Short Q 嘅出題方式。
  • Application: The Live-In Rule Challenge (應用：外傭強制同住規則 · Q13)
    Q13 applies the human rights framework to a live issue: the requirement that foreign domestic helpers live in their employer's home. FDHs are well informed of this arrangement before coming to Hong Kong, and the legal challenge to the rule ultimately failed — the case shows the limits of human rights arguments where immigration law and unincorporated treaty rights are involved.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    答 Q13 要用返上面嘅框架分析：訴求方係弱者（外傭）、掌權者係入境處處長；但 ICESCR 權利未經立法本地化，加上出入境保留條款，挑戰最終失敗，同住要求維持。
    📌 Case: Lubiano Nancy Almorin v Director of Immigration (CA, 21 September 2020, CACV 112/2018) — Q13
    Facts: A foreign domestic helper challenged the Live-In Requirement — the rule that the Director of Immigration grants an FDH employment visa only if she undertakes to reside at her employer's residence. She argued the rule heightened the risk that FDHs would be forced to work on rest days, violating the ICESCR Article 7(b) and (d) rights to safe and healthy working conditions, adequate rest and limited working hours. The Court of First Instance dismissed her judicial review in 2018; she appealed.
    Holding: The Court of Appeal dismissed the appeal and upheld the CFI. The ICESCR Article 7 right was not given domestic effect by the Employment Ordinance (Cap 57): treaty rights become enforceable only through an act of domestication by the legislature (the EO s.17 rest-day entitlement in fact predated the ICESCR's application to Hong Kong). The immigration reservation in the Bill of Rights Ordinance (Cap 383) s.11 applied, and the Live-In Requirement also fell within the ICESCR Article 6 reservation, being designed to safeguard employment opportunities for local non-live-in domestic helpers. On causation, the applicant failed the high threshold: if an employer ill-treats an FDH, the real cause is the employer, not the live-in arrangement. The Live-In Requirement therefore remains in force.
    🇭🇰 中文：四個敗因要齊：① ICESCR 第 7 條權利未經立法本地化，僱傭條例唔等於實施咗佢；② 《人權法案條例》第 11 條出入境保留適用；③ 第 6 條保留涵蓋以保障本地家務工就業為目的嘅規則；④ 因果唔成立 —— 虐待嘅真正成因係僱主，唔係同住安排本身。

L9 Kong Yun-ming 案例：司法覆核與福利權案例必背
  • The Case: Facts & Procedural History (案件事實與程序)
    Kong Yunming v Director of Social Welfare is the leading Hong Kong case on the constitutional right to social welfare. Kong challenged, by judicial review, the seven-year residence requirement for CSSA introduced on 1 January 2004 — one of two challenges arising from the Task Force on Population Policy Report 2003. The Court of First Instance upheld the requirement in 2009 and the Court of Appeal agreed; the Court of Final Appeal reversed them (FACV 2/2013).
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    程序次序係畀分位：CFI（2009）維持 7 年要求 → 上訴庭維持 → CFA 推翻並裁定違憲。唔好寫成「法庭一開始就判政府輸」。
    📌 Case: Kong Yunming v Director of Social Welfare (CFA, FACV 2/2013)
    Facts: Kong Yunming, a mainlander born in 1949, met her husband (a Hong Kong permanent resident born in 1929) in 2001 and married him in 2003, when he was 74 and she was 54. He had been a CSSA recipient since 1985. She was granted a one-way permit on 30 November 2005 to join him, and arrived in Hong Kong on 21 December 2005, aged 56. Her husband, aged 76, died the following day, 22 December 2005. The Housing Authority repossessed his public housing flat, leaving her homeless and admitted to a shelter for street sleepers. When she applied for CSSA on 20 March 2006, she was refused: she had not met the seven-year residence requirement, and the Director of Social Welfare did not exercise his discretion to waive it in her case. She was granted legal aid and brought judicial review proceedings.
    Holding: The CFA unanimously allowed the appeal and declared the seven-year residence requirement unconstitutional. The requirement was not rationally connected to the Government's declared aim of ensuring the sustainability of the social security system, and was manifestly without reasonable foundation.
    🇭🇰 中文：事實要寫齊數字同日期：1949 年出生、2003 年結婚、丈夫自 1985 年領綜援、2005 年 12 月 21 日持單程證抵港、翌日丈夫去世、公屋被收回、2006 年申請綜援被拒、署長冇行使酌情豁免。呢啲事實正正係 CFA 同情同法理嘅基礎。
  • CSSA: Purpose & History (綜援：目的與歷史)
    The Comprehensive Social Security Assistance (CSSA) Scheme is a safety net for those who cannot support themselves financially, for reasons such as age or family circumstances, meeting basic needs such as food and shelter. The residence requirement has tightened and loosened over time: a 10-year requirement in 1948; reduced to 5 years in 1959; a 1-year requirement proposed in a 1970 Executive Council memorandum; in 1993 Public Assistance became CSSA; and in 2004 the requirement was raised to 7 years — the change Kong challenged.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    年份位要背齊：1948 十年 → 1959 五年 → 1970 行會備忘錄建議一年 → 1993 公共援助改名綜援 → 2004 七年。歷史顯示 7 年唔係一向如此，而係 2004 年突然收緊，先至有違憲挑戰嘅空間。
  • Constitutional Provisions (憲法條文)
    Kong relied on: Basic Law Article 36 — Hong Kong residents shall have the right to social welfare in accordance with law, and the welfare benefits and retirement security of the labour force shall be protected by law; Basic Law Article 25 — all Hong Kong residents shall be equal before the law; and Bill of Rights Article 22 — rights enjoyed without discrimination. Against her stood Basic Law Article 145: the HKSAR Government shall, on its own, formulate policies on the development and improvement of the social welfare system in the light of economic conditions and social needs. The case is therefore a contest between a constitutional right (Art 36) and a governmental policy power (Art 145).
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    成案嘅骨架係 Art 36（福利權）對 Art 145（政策制訂權）：政府話福利政策由政府因應經濟同社會需要制訂；Kong 話 Art 36 係受憲法保障嘅權利，限制佢要過到比例審查。Art 25 同人權法案 Art 22 係平等／不歧視嘅輔助理據。
  • The CFA's Proportionality Analysis (終院判決：比例原則 · paras 36–37)
    At paras 36–37 the CFA held that Article 36 protects a real right to social welfare — on the Q12 analysis, the right to CSSA benefits under the scheme as it stood on 1 July 1997, with a qualifying condition of one year's residence — while Article 145 empowers the Government to formulate policies that may modify, reduce or even eliminate benefits. The tension is resolved this way: any restriction on the Art 36 right is subject to constitutional review by proportionality analysis. As formulated in the case, the test asks: (1) is the restriction pursuing a legitimate societal aim? (2) is it rationally connected to that aim? and (3) is it manifestly without reasonable foundation?
    Two further findings matter. At para 44, the seven-year requirement removed the safety net Kong would otherwise have had: she was left living below the living standard defined by the Director of Social Welfare himself. At para 46, the requirement did not apply to all new arrivals — there were exceptional cases and the Director's discretion — which undercut the claim that a uniform seven-year bar was necessary.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    段號係高分位：paras 36–37 定框架（Art 36 有權利、限制要過比例審查）；para 44 講後果（安全網被抽走，生活低過署方自訂嘅基本標準）；para 46 講一致性（本身有例外同酌情，一刀切七年好難話係必要）。
  • Legitimate Aim: Why "Saving Money" Failed (合法目的：為何「慳錢」唔成立 · Section G)
    A legitimate societal aim must further the legitimate interests of society. The CFA held that the Government's claim to save money is not, by itself, a legitimate aim: both the purpose and the effect of the expenditure cut must be considered. The sustainability claim was examined at para 53 — built on the one-way permit scheme, the ageing population and the rise in CSSA expenditure — but it did not hold up. At Section H2, the court noted that family union under the one-way permit scheme is the main source of population growth.
    The CFA's stance was that the seven-year requirement ran against two of the Government's own social policies: the one-way permit family union policy, and the population policy of rejuvenating the ageing population. The savings achieved were insignificant, CSSA spending overall had increased, and the Director had reported in 2003 that 18% of new arrivals were on CSSA. On that evidence, the requirement could not be justified by sustainability.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    呢段係成案勝負關鍵，要寫齊三層：① 慳錢本身唔係合法目的，要睇埋削減嘅目的同效果；② 7 年要求同單程證家庭團聚、人口年輕化兩項政府自己嘅政策自相矛盾；③ 實際慳到嘅錢微不足道，綜援總開支反而上升。
  • Other Justifications Considered (其他理據 · Sections K & L)
    The Government's further arguments (Section K) also failed: the complaint that non-contributory, heavily subsidised benefits lacked coherence; the plea at para 102 for uniformity of qualifying periods across benefits; the point that new arrivals contribute to the economy, for example by looking after children (95% of new arrivals on CSSA were women); and the unclear impact of the fiscal deficit. Nor did the matters in Section L save the requirement: that prior warning of the change had been given, or that charities and the Director's discretion could fill the gap. The basis of the decision is therefore precise: the seven-year requirement was not rationally connected to its declared aim of ensuring sustainability.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    結論要寫準：CFA 唔係話「政府唔可以慳錢」，而係話呢個 7 年要求同「確保可持續」呢個目的之間冇合理關連（not rationally connected），所以過唔到比例測試。寫錯敗因成段失分。
  • Karen Kong (2012): A Critique of the Approach (學者批評)
    Karen Kong (2012) argues that the court's internal restraints on adjudicating socio-economic rights are misplaced: there is room for a more principled approach, while still respecting the democratically elected government's freedom to determine its policies. Her central point is that Article 36 is a self-standing right — yet the judgment reads as if the Art 36 right hinges on Art 145, so that the welfare right risks being reduced to whatever the policy power leaves behind.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    Short Q 問「評價判決」嗰陣用呢段：Karen Kong 唔係話結果錯，而係話推理太保守 —— Art 36 應該係獨立權利，唔應該睇落好似要依附 Art 145 先存在。引學者批評係批判思考嘅高分位。
  • Comparison: Yao Man Fai George v DSW (2010) (對照案例)
    The second challenge arising from the same 2004 changes attacked a different requirement: the one-year continuous residence requirement. The contrast between the two cases — different requirement, different applicant, different court outcome at first instance — is a favourite comparison question.
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    唔好撈亂兩案：Kong 挑戰「居港滿 7 年」要求（新來港者）；Yao 挑戰「連續居港 1 年」要求（返港嘅永久居民）。要求唔同、申請人身份唔同、援引條文都唔同。
    📌 Case: Yao Man Fai George v Director of Social Welfare (2010)
    Facts: Yao, a Hong Kong permanent resident, had his employment in the Mainland terminated and returned to Hong Kong. He applied for CSSA but was rejected because he did not satisfy the one-year continuous residence requirement.
    Holding: The Court of First Instance declared the requirement unconstitutional. It infringed Basic Law Article 25 (equality before the law) and Article 31 (freedom to travel): discrimination based on the period of absence was unjustifiable, and the Government failed to show a genuine need for the requirement — only 4.7% of CSSA applicants fell into this category, and the majority of them obtained a waiver anyway.
    🇭🇰 中文：Yao 嘅殺著係數據：受影響者只佔申請人 4.7%，大多數仲獲豁免 —— 政府證明唔到真正需要，區別對待就唔成立。條文要齊：Art 25 平等 + Art 31 遷徙自由。
  • Karen Kong's Seven Questions (評估居港要求嘅七條問題)
    Karen Kong proposes seven questions for assessing whether a residence requirement goes no more than necessary. They convert the proportionality test into a working checklist for evaluating welfare restrictions:
    • (1) What is the Government's assessment of compliance with economic, social and cultural rights?
    • (2) How serious is the limitation on the dignity and livelihood of the disadvantaged group?
    • (3) Is there special concern for those most gravely affected?
    • (4) What is the financial implication of the requirement?
    • (5) Were alternative options considered — for example, gradual steps rather than a one-off change?
    • (6) Is there a mechanism for periodic review and monitoring?
    • (7) Was there sufficient public consultation?
    🇭🇰 中文導讀：
    七條問題係現成嘅分析框架：Short Q 畀任何新嘅福利限制措施，都可以逐條套用 —— 特別係 (2) 對弱勢尊嚴同生計嘅影響、(5) 有冇較溫和嘅替代方案（漸進 vs 一刀切）、(7) 諮詢是否足夠。
Short Q 出題陷阱 / 高分位 — L9 Kong Yun-ming
  • 先定框架：Art 36 係福利權（以 1997 年 7 月 1 日綜援計劃、一年居港期為內容），Art 145 只係政策制訂權；限制權利要過 proportionality，唔好將兩條倒轉。
  • 比例測試寫齊三步：legitimate aim → rationally connected → not manifestly without reasonable foundation；段號要準：paras 36–37（框架）、44（抽走安全網）、46（酌情例外）、53（可持續性）、102（劃一資格期）。
  • 「慳錢」陷阱：saving money 本身唔係 legitimate aim，要睇埋削減嘅 purpose and effect；加上慳款微不足道、綜援開支反升，先至得出 not rationally connected 嘅結論。
  • 政策衝突位係關鍵理由：7 年要求同時牴觸單程證家庭團聚政策同 rejuvenate the ageing population 人口政策 —— 係政府自己嘅政策打自己。
  • 兩案唔好撈亂：Kong 係 7 年要求，CFI 2009 維持、CFA 推翻；Yao Man Fai 係 1 年連續居港，CFI 已裁違憲（Art 25 + Art 31，受影響者只佔 4.7%）。
  • 高分位：引 Karen Kong (2012) 批評（Art 36 係 self-standing right，唔應依附 Art 145）同佢七條問題做評估框架，顯示批判思考。
L10–L12 補充：私隱／起底・性騷擾・專業操守・強制舉報未有 slides · 靠 Q17–Q20
  • PDPO Cap 486 六大原則 DPP：1 收集目的及方式（合法公平、必要不過量、要通知）2 準確及保留期限 3 使用（只限原目的）4 保安 5 透明（資料政策公開）6 查閱及改正。
  • Eastweek v Privacy Commissioner (1999) CA：街頭偷拍相片刊登 — 上訴庭推翻原訟庭：決定有冇違反 DPP1(2)(b) 要兩元素齊 — ① 有「收集個人資料」行為 ② 手段不公平。本案裁定根本無「收集」呢個行為，DPP1 唔被觸發，專員決定被撤銷。
  • 2021 起底修訂（背景：2019 社會事件）：未經同意披露個人資料 + 意圖或罔顧造成指明傷害 — 一級（簡易）$100,000 + 2 年；二級（公訴）$1,000,000 + 5 年；保護埋家人；私隱專員獲刑事調查、檢控及發停止通知權力。
  • 性騷擾 SDO Cap 480 s.2(5)：向女性作出不受歡迎嘅性行進／性要求，或其他不受歡迎涉及性嘅行徑，而合理人會預期佢感到受冒犯、侮辱或威嚇；s.2(7)「涉及性嘅行徑」包括口頭或書面涉及性嘅陳述。s.39(3)：學生性騷擾同校女性屬違法。Yuen Sha Sha v Tse Chi Pan (1998)：課室裝 camcorder 偷拍更衣 — 感情損害賠償 $50,000 + 加重 $10,000 + 懲罰性 $20,000 + 書面道歉（s.76)。Cheng Yin Fong 案：原告只係屋苑訪客，對管理公司等無企位 locus standi — 無案可答。
  • 專業操守（Q18）：《社工工作守則》第 14 段 — 社工喺任何情況下都唔可以同服務使用者有任何性活動或性接觸，不論自願或被迫（14.1 唔可性交、14.2 唔可愛撫刺激性器官）。Lee Kwok Wah 案：註冊局按 Cap 505 s.25(1)(a) 經紀律研訊後永久除名。預防：唔單獨見服務使用者、保持距離、CCTV、舉報機制、加強監管。
  • 非禮 vs 強姦（成日撈亂）：非禮 s.122 — 施襲者可男可女、行為由觸摸到非插入式性侵犯、最高 10 年、16 歲以下不能合法同意；強姦 s.118 — 只限男性、必須陰莖插入陰道（最輕微插入已足夠、唔使射精）、最高終身。
  • 強制舉報虐兒 Cap 650（2026 年 1 月生效）：25 類指明專業人員（含註冊社工、教師、醫護、臨床／教育心理學家）喺工作期間有合理理由懷疑兒童（<18 歲）正遭受嚴重傷害或有實質風險，要喺切實可行範圍內盡快向社署署長或警務處處長舉報。例外：純意外（非疏忽造成）、兒童自傷、其他兒童造成（非性質行為）、社署／警方已通知同一事件、合理相信另一指明人員已報。唔報：簡易定罪罰款第 5 級 $50,000；公訴再加監禁 3 個月（s.5 有法定抗辯）。保護：阻礙舉報或披露舉報人身份屬罪行（s.9/11）；舉報本身免民事刑事責任同專業守則追究（s.12)。
